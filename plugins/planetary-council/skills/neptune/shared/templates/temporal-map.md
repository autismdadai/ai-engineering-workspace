# Saturn Temporal Map (Mandatory)

Every significant institutional or policy recommendation must be examined at three timescales:

| Horizon | Question |
|---------|----------|
| **5 years** | What looks successful or harmless in the near term? |
| **50 years** | What cultural, institutional, and power consequences emerge? |
| **500 years** | What civilisational path does this make easier or harder? |

## Justice Test (all three required)
- Procedural justice (fair process)
- Substantive justice (fair outcome)
- Intergenerational justice (fair to the unborn)

## Structural Load Rule
Never remove a load-bearing institution, law, or norm without designing its replacement first.
