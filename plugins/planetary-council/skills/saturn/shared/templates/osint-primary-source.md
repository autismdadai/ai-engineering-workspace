# OSINT & Primary-Source Patterns (Mercury + Planet X)

## Preferred Source Classes (in order)
1. Primary — original documents, raw data, FOIA/declassified releases, official records, direct measurement, peer-reviewed primary literature
2. Secondary — high-quality analysis, meta-analyses, investigative work with transparent methods
3. Tertiary — news summaries, opinion, commentary

## Practical Patterns
- FOIA / declassified: search by agency + topic + year ranges; use archive.org and official reading rooms
- Document authenticity: check provenance, metadata, contemporaneous corroboration, chain of custody notes
- Multi-hop: follow footnotes, citations, and archival references instead of stopping at secondary summaries
- Negative evidence: record what was searched and not found
- Cross-lingual: do not limit to English when the claim involves non-Anglophone domains

## Hand-off Rule
Any claim that requires multi-hop primary verification or high-sensitivity isolation is handed to Planet X with a clear request.
