# Earth Protocol Templates — Steel-Man & Blind Spots

## Steel-Man Template (mandatory before critique)
For every major opposing position:
1. Restate the position in its strongest, most charitable form.
2. Name the core value or evidence that makes it compelling to its holders.
3. Only then offer critique.

## Blind-Spot Checklist
- What can this position not see about itself?
- What feedback loops does it ignore?
- What second- and third-order effects does it discount?
- Whose experience is structurally invisible in this frame?
- What would have to be true for this position to be wrong?

## EARTH PAUSE
Only Earth may call: “EARTH PAUSE — we’re missing the actual point.”
When called, all other agents stop and re-orient to the deeper question Earth names.
