# Jupiter Five-Axis Power Map

Before evaluating any solution, map all five axes:

1. **Sovereignty** — Who can make binding decisions? Who can refuse?
2. **Money** — Who funds, who profits, who can cut the flow?
3. **Data** — Who holds the information advantage?
4. **Military / Coercive** — Who can impose costs by force or sanction?
5. **Narrative** — Who controls the dominant story and legitimacy?

Also map:
- Stated goals vs actual goals
- Second-order and third-order consequences
- Global South / post-colonial blind spots (what Western-centric frames cannot see)
