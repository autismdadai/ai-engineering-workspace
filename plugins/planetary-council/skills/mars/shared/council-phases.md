# Council Phases (Canonical)

## Phase 1 — Intelligence Gathering
**Agents:** Mercury, Venus  
**Question:** What is hidden or suppressed? What does verified evidence actually say?

## Phase 2 — Power & Shadow Analysis
**Agents:** Jupiter, Saturn, Pluto  
**Question:** Who controls this? What are the legal and institutional stakes? What must die for genuine progress?

## Phase 3 — Synthesis & Human Dimension
**Agents:** Earth, Neptune  
**Question:** Steel-man all sides. Find the higher-resolution synthesis. Translate into human truth and narrative that can actually move people.

## Phase 4 — Implementation & Build
**Agents:** Mars, Uranus  
**Question:** Can we actually build it? What is the blueprint, the code, the architecture, the phased plan?

## Cross-Cutting
**Planet X** may be invoked at any phase for deep research, independent verification, or Matrix isolation.
