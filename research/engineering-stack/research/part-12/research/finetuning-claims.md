## (a) The 10 facts the writer most needs

1. **Hosted fine-tuning has mostly closed.** OpenAI is "winding down the fine-tuning platform": closed to organizations that never fine-tuned since May 7, 2026, and to all customers for new jobs on January 6, 2027 (F065, F066). The Gemini API has had no tunable model since May 2025 (F072). Mistral marks its fine-tuning deprecated (F049). The only Claude offer found was Claude 3 Haiku in Amazon Bedrock, and that model left Anthropic's API on April 20, 2026 (F073, F074). For a reader in late 2026, fine-tuning means open-weight models and local or rented hardware.
2. **For new facts, retrieve; do not fine-tune.** On 910 questions about events after training, RAG on the base model beat fine-tuning on paraphrased text by 19.3 to 31.0 percentage points across three 7B models (Ovadia et al., EMNLP 2024; F030 to F033). Facts the model did not know are learned slowly and, once learned, raise hallucination; early stopping or dropping those examples avoids most of the harm (Gekhman et al., EMNLP 2024, PaLM 2-S; F034 to F037). Vendor pages that say fine-tuning teaches new facts (Mistral F050, the HF course F026) give no evidence.
3. **Prompt first.** OpenAI ("The prompt engineering process may be all you need"), Hugging Face's course and Mistral all advise starting with prompting and evals (F026, F050, F070).
4. **LoRA's trade-off is measured.** With Llama-2-7B, LoRA nearly matched full fine-tuning for instruction tuning at rank 256 but lagged by 3.9 to 9.0 points in continued pretraining, and it forgot less (Biderman et al., venue unconfirmed; F038 to F041). Their advice (all modules, rank up to 256, alpha = 2r, higher learning rate) differs from PEFT's defaults (r 8, alpha 8, q and v only; F024).
5. **Fine-tuning can undo safety, even by accident.** Ten harmful examples took GPT-3.5 Turbo's harmfulness rate from 1.8% to 88.8% for under $0.20 in 2023; one epoch on benign Alpaca data took it from 5.5% to 31.8% (Qi et al., arXiv v1; F042 to F045). Re-run safety and quality evals after every fine-tune.
6. **Memory arithmetic.** Full fine-tuning with Adam in mixed precision costs 16 bytes per parameter before activations (ZeRO; 7B gives 112 GB; F063, F064). LoRA trains r x (d + k) parameters per matrix (F053); on GPT-3 175B it cut training memory from 1.2 TB to 350 GB and the checkpoint from 350 GB to 35 MB (ICLR 2022; F052 to F056). QLoRA fits 65B on one 48 GB GPU with NF4, double quantization (0.373 bits per parameter saved) and paged optimizers (NeurIPS 2023; F057 to F061).
7. **Telemetry is on by default in three of the libraries.** TRL pings Hugging Face once per trainer (F010, F011); Axolotl sends PostHog events (F012, F013); Unsloth downloads "statistics" repos on every model load (F009); LLaMA-Factory's web UI inherits Gradio's analytics (F079). `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` stops TRL and Axolotl but not Unsloth (`UNSLOTH_DISABLE_STATISTICS`) or Gradio (`GRADIO_ANALYTICS_ENABLED=False`).
8. **Licences are not what the labels say.** Unsloth's metadata says Apache-2.0, but 81 of 159 files in its core package carry AGPL-3.0 headers, and its required unsloth_zoo is LGPL-3.0-or-later with AGPL files (F007, F008). PEFT, TRL, Axolotl and LLaMA-Factory are Apache-2.0; MLX-LM and llama.cpp MIT; torchtune BSD-3 (F001 to F006, F016). Llama 3 banned using outputs to improve other LLMs; Llama 3.1 and later instead require "Llama" at the start of any shared model trained on Llama outputs (F047, F048). OpenAI and Anthropic terms bar using outputs to build competing models (F075, F076). Not legal advice.
9. **What runs where.** Unsloth needs a GPU (or MLX on Apple silicon) and refuses plain CPUs (F018); Axolotl wants NVIDIA or AMD, with partial Mac support (F077); MLX-LM does LoRA, DoRA, QLoRA and full fine-tuning on Apple silicon, with one measured figure of about 250 tokens per second for Mistral 7B on an M1 Max with 32 GB (F019 to F021); transformers' Trainer (under PEFT and TRL) supports CPU with `use_cpu=True` and Apple's MPS (F080, F081). llama.cpp's finetune is a work-in-progress, FP32, full-weight experiment, not LoRA (F015, F016). torchtune is no longer maintained (F006).
10. **Versions clash.** Axolotl 0.21.0 pins trl 1.14.1; Unsloth 2026.10.7 caps trl at 1.15.0 and transformers at 5.17.0; LLaMA-Factory 0.9.5 caps peft at 0.18.1 and trl at 0.24.0. One virtual environment per library (F014). Data formats differ too: TRL's prompt/chosen/rejected, OpenAI's input/preferred_output/non_preferred_output, MLX-LM's chat/tools/completions/text, LLaMA-Factory's alpaca/sharegpt; templates must match the model's chat template (F022, F027, F028, F069, F078, F082).
