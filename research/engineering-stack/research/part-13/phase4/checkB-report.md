## Problems found, in order of importance
1. B50: arithmetic is wrong (10,000/86,400 = 0.116, not 0.93; the 7-streams figure follows from the wrong rate).
2. B40: FastAPI built-in OpenTelemetry arrived in 0.142.0; 0.143.0 only made auto-setup opt-in.
3. B54: "Anthropic 429 with no retry-after pages a person" overgeneralises; only the spend-cap 429 lacks it and keeps failing.
4. B68/B75: only some Gemini prices double on Jan 1, 2027; another model changes Dec 1, 2026.
5. B26/B67: whole-request repricing is stated by OpenAI (gpt-6-astra) and, for Haiku 5.5 only, Anthropic; Gemini's page is silent; other Anthropic models have no long-context surcharge.
6. B38/B64: the WHATWG "delays in event dispatch" line is about user-agent buffering, not proxy buffering.
7. B8, B14, B18 (Weave is error reports, not usage), B20/B44 (Phoenix does evaluate production traces; AX not called paid), B24 (2x is GPT-5.6 Sol only), B53, B30/B43/B76 (2023 to 2024 scope not in the sources).
