## (c) Changes the picture (writer must not miss)

1. Ollama is not quiet on the network by default. Every server start lists cloud models from ollama.com, and model recommendations are fetched every 4 hours, unless OLLAMA_NO_CLOUD=1 or `disable_ollama_cloud` is set (Q014, Q015, Q011). The Windows and macOS apps also check ollama.com/api/update every hour and no setting stops the check; the "Auto-download updates" toggle (default on) only stops downloads (Q002-Q008, Q010). "Zero telemetry, air-gapped" must go. Safe wording: prompts to local models stay on the machine (Q018), no analytics library was found (Q017), and full offline use means OLLAMA_NO_CLOUD=1 plus a firewall, with models pulled beforehand.
2. Ollama v0.40.5 runs llama.cpp's own llama-server (pinned build b11669) as its engine (Q023). Present Ollama as a model manager and service wrapper around llama.cpp, not a separate runtime.
3. Ollama's default context is tiered by GPU memory (4096 below 23 GiB VRAM, 32768 from 23 GiB, 262144 from 47 GiB), and its own FAQ still says a flat 4096 (Q020, Q021). For the CPU-only 8 GB reader, 4096 is right.
4. "4-bit" Q4_K_M is a mixture averaging about 4.9 bits/weight (Q078, Q083). On Llama 3 8B it adds 2.79% perplexity, the top token changes at about 8.1% of positions, and 1% of tokens lose at least 19.6 points of probability (Q091, Q095, Q096). Benchmark averages barely move (-0.32 points on Llama 3.1 8B Instruct, Q103), but NeurIPS 2024 shows accuracy can hide answer flips (Q099), and small models and low-resource languages lose more (Q102). Prefer "small, measurable loss that varies by model and task" over "minimal".
5. Cite GPTQ's ICLR 2023 paper by its published title "OPTQ" (Q127). AWQ is MLSys 2024 (Q126).
6. Tool status changed: AutoAWQ deprecated in favour of vLLM's llm-compressor (Q117), AutoGPTQ unmaintained in favour of GPTQModel (Q119), ExLlamaV2 archived in favour of ExLlamaV3 (Q120, Q121). bitsandbytes 0.50.2 now has a CPU backend needing AVX2 (Q124, Q125).
7. llama.cpp changed its front door: a unified `llama` command, a llama.app installer that downloads from a Hugging Face bucket into ~/.local/bin with no checksum (Q146, Q147, Q150), and llama-server's default port is now 9931, not 8080 (Q152).
8. Licences for small models differ: Qwen3, Qwen3.5, SmolLM3, Granite 4 and Gemma 4 are Apache 2.0; Phi-4-mini is MIT; Gemma 3 uses Google's Gemma Terms; Llama 3.2 has Meta's custom community licence (Q128, Q132, Q135, Q137, Q138, Q140, Q142, Q144). LM Studio itself is proprietary (Q158).
9. On an 8 GB CPU-only PC with Intel UHD 630: UHD 630 ships with 8th to 10th generation Core chips, below the SYCL floor of 11th generation (Q049, Q050), and Ollama ignores Intel iGPUs unless OLLAMA_IGPU_ENABLE=1 (Q042). Plan for CPU-only. llama.cpp and Ollama ship a baseline no-AVX CPU variant (Q043, Q044), while LM Studio on Windows requires AVX2 (Q161).
10. gpt-oss-20b is not "4-bit everywhere": only MoE expert weights are MXFP4 (4.25 bits/weight: 32 E2M1 values plus one E8M0 scale per block), while attention, router and embeddings stay BF16; the checkpoint is 12.8 GiB with 20.91B total and 3.61B active parameters (Q107, Q111-Q115). The 16 GB figure is OpenAI's own.
