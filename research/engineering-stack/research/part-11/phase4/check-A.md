## How to read this file

- Quotes. Text in double quotation marks or backticks is copied from the file or page I read, with these exceptions: line breaks inside a quote were joined, markdown markup and trademark symbols were dropped, and where code or a comment wraps over several lines I quote it as one string. Quoted commit messages come from `git log`, and quoted file names inside wheels from the archive listing, not from file contents. I checked every quoted string of 14 characters or more in the evidence column by machine, against the files and the saved WebFetch results; the strings that do not match letter for letter differ only in the ways listed here (A032, A034, A048, A058, A078, A081, A086, A098 for wrapping or markup; A084 and A090 for commit messages). Three dots join two pieces of the same passage. A backslash before a bar (\|) is the table-safe way to write a bar that is in the source. Where a source prints an en dash or em dash inside a quoted passage (for example the vLLM paper's range of 1.7 to 2.7 times, printed with an en dash), I wrote a hyphen or the word "to", because this file may contain neither dash; the row says so where it matters. Words outside quotation marks are mine.
- Web sources. Pages and papers were read with WebFetch, which passes the page to a small model that summarises it. That model refuses to quote more than 125 characters at a time, so many web quotes here are fragments; it can miss text or cut a long page short; and a NOT FOUND from it is weaker evidence than a quote it returned. Rows that rest on a NOT FOUND say so. Git repositories and PyPI files were read directly, not through WebFetch.
- Arithmetic. Every sum, ratio and unit conversion in the claims was recomputed with python3 (the block-byte and bits-per-weight figures in A014 and A036 to A046, and the numbers in A002, A026, A029 to A031, A033, A034, A050, A051, A053, A054, A062, A070, A072, A079 and A080). "My count" in a row means my own computation, not a figure from the source.
- Lookups. 45 of the 45 allowed web lookups were used (section (d)). I spent the last ones on the points that decided a verdict; the few points I could not reach within the budget are named in the rows and in the "Not opened" list in section (d).

Note on blindness: while listing the working folder I opened the first five lines of a sibling file in the same folder (tester-sections.md; it only showed a heading and a date line). I made no use of it.

## (c) The 10 most important problems, worst first

Ranking rule: first, a detail the named source contradicts; then a specific fact (price, date, licence, mechanism) that the named sources do not contain; then a statement that holds only in part of its scope. Each item says where the evidence is and what to change.

1. A077 (partly supported). The claim puts vLLM's example in chunks of 1, 2 and 2 tokens. vLLM's docs/benchmarking/cli.md (lines 158 to 160) says "The three tokens in the second streamed output do not create additional ITL samples", and its figure latency-metrics-speculative-decoding-light.svg labels Output 1 = T1, Output 2 (bundled) = T2 T3 T4, Output 3 = T5. So the chunks are 1, 3 and 1. TPOT 20 ms and ITL 40 ms are right. Fix: write "chunks of 1, 3 and 1 tokens", or call the 1, 2, 2 version the lab's own example.
