## Open questions (not settled)

1. GPT-6 tokenizer: tiktoken's model map (main branch) has no gpt-6 entry and no OpenAI page I opened names the GPT-6 encoding. Use POST /v1/responses/input_tokens for exact counts, or treat o200k_base counts for GPT-6 as unverified estimates.
2. Current-model position effect, measured directly: no peer-reviewed or vendor-independent study I opened tests GPT-6, Claude 5.x or Gemini 3.x for lost-in-the-middle. The newest evidence is Chroma (Jul 2025; Claude 4, GPT-4.1, o3, Gemini 2.5), Veseli et al. (Aug 2025 preprint), Esmi et al. (Feb 2026; GPT-5, claim of "largely resolved" without a method in the abstract) and Ebrahimzadeh & Salili (Jul 2026; GPT-5-mini, Claude 4.5 Haiku, Gemini 2.5 Flash; spread-out evidence hurts).
3. Anthropic's "up to 30 percent" claim gives no model, benchmark or date. Probably carried over from Claude 2/3-era docs; not checked.
4. TACL year for Liu et al.: the arXiv page says TACL but I did not open the TACL page to confirm volume and year (commonly cited as TACL 2024, vol. 12).
5. RULER venue (COLM 2024) is not shown on the arXiv abstract page.
6. Michelangelo task names, models and lengths not checked (abstract only). Fiction.LiveBench current scores not found on an official page.
7. OpenAI: whether /v1/responses/input_tokens is free, and whether Responses compaction is GA or beta: not stated on the pages opened.
8. Gemini pricing page: last ~25K characters unread; a length tier on other models could sit there.

## Where sources disagree

- Placement for a single instruction block: OpenAI GPT-4.1 guide says if instructions appear once, above the context beats below. Anthropic (A24-A25) and Google (A33) say put the query at the end, after the long documents. OpenAI's preferred option (both ends) matches Liu et al. §4.2 query-aware contextualization, which helped key-value retrieval but not multi-doc QA. Note "instructions" vs "query" may not mean the same thing across vendors.
- Words per token: OpenAI 100 tokens ~ 75 words; Google 100 tokens ~ 60-80 words; Anthropic gives no ratio but says its 4.7+ tokenizer yields ~30% more tokens for the same text. A single rule of thumb does not transfer across vendors.
- Ollama default context: 4k/32k/256k by VRAM (context-length page) vs 4096 (FAQ) vs 2048 (Modelfile table). The context-length page is the most specific; the others look stale.
- Position effect, current models: Chroma found none in its NIAH test but did find one in repeated-words; NoLiMa found a middle dip at 32K for one-hop but length dominated for two-hop; Veseli et al. find the U-shape fades into a recency bias past 50% of the window; Esmi et al. say it is "largely resolved". The fair summary: the classic U-shape is weaker in newer models on simple single-fact retrieval, but length-driven degradation (context rot) remains, and is worse when many facts or reasoning are needed.
- Overflow behavior differs by stack: Anthropic errors (400) or stops with model_context_window_exceeded; OpenAI errors (400) by default and drops oldest items only with truncation "auto"; Ollama silently drops oldest chat messages by default.
- Anthropic compaction headers: on-demand uses compact-2026-09-04; threshold uses compact-2026-01-12. Both current, not a contradiction, but easy to mix up.
