- **B4-01** (cloud APIs a "non-starter" for medical, defense, finance): earlier verdict **holds, corrected**. Defense and finance still not checked.
- **B4-08** (local summarisation avoids HIPAA violation via zero network transmission): earlier verdict **holds, corrected**. HHS Security Rule summary (last reviewed Aug 7, 2026) covers ePHI created, received, maintained or transmitted, and requires administrative, physical and technical safeguards plus risk analysis. Local processing removes one vendor risk but does not by itself make the setup compliant.
