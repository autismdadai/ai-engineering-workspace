## Claims

A001. [1. In plain words] MCP is a shared plug: a **server** offers tools (and data and prompts), an **AI application** (the **host**) connects to it through a **client**, and they talk in JSON-RPC, a simple format for requests and replies .  SOURCES: MSP
A002. [1. In plain words] Its first revision is dated 2024-11-05 ; on December 9, 2025 Anthropic donated it to the Agentic AI Foundation, a fund under the Linux Foundation .  SOURCES: AAF, MGT
A003. [1. In plain words] The A2A spec says the two protocols are complementary: MCP connects an agent to "tools, APIs, data sources", A2A lets agents "communicate and collaborate with each other as peers" .  SOURCES: A2S
A004. [1. In plain words] The agent reads only the names and descriptions at the start, and opens a skill's full text when a task matches .  SOURCES: ASK
A005. [1. In plain words] The format began at Anthropic and is now an open specification read by Claude, OpenAI's Codex, GitHub Copilot, Google's Gemini CLI and Cursor, among others .  SOURCES: ASK, CDX, CUR, GCS, GHC
A006. [1. In plain words] Anthropic counted 58 tools from five MCP servers at about 55,000 tokens .  SOURCES: AAT
A007. [1. In plain words] Every server you connect is also code you trust: published attacks hide instructions in tool descriptions, and real vulnerabilities have been found in popular MCP tooling (Section 3.11) .  SOURCES: C49, C65, INV
